Teaching

As a lab associate at IST (2023–2024), I taught observational astronomy and telescope operation to undergraduate space science students and ran their practical observing sessions. In 2024 I lectured on mechanics, optics and modern physics at Government Graduate College Chichawatni.

My simulators grew out of this teaching, as a way to let students see a transit or a phase curve rather than just read about one.

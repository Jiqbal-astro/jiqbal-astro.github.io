Tools

Two browser-based simulators I wrote for teaching and outreach. Both run in any web browser with nothing to install, and I have used them in classes and public talks since 2023.

Exoplanet transit simulator

Models planetary transits and draws the resulting light curve, including limb darkening. You can change the orbit, add more planets, show the habitable zone, and compare your setup with real systems such as TRAPPIST-1e and Kepler-186f.

Open the simulator

Lunar and planetary phase simulator

Shows how a moon or planet looks as it moves around its orbit and plots the reflected-light phase curve. Phases can be tied to a real date, inclination and eccentricity are adjustable, and results can be saved as PNG or CSV.
